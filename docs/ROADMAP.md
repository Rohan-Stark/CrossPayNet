# CrossPayNet Roadmap

This roadmap covers Phase 0 through Phase 13 of the project's lifecycle. It is a planning baseline, not an irreversible architecture. Future phases may be divided into subphases (e.g., Phase 2.A, Phase 2.B) as complexity dictates.

* **Phase 0** — Engineering Constitution
* **Phase 1** — Architecture & Domain Modeling
* **Phase 2** — Banking Foundation
* **Phase 3** — Double-Entry Financial Ledger
* **Phase 4** — Payment Engine
* **Phase 5** — Financial Messaging / ISO 20022-inspired Messaging
* **Phase 6** — Routing & Correspondent Banking
* **Phase 7** — Distributed Systems, Kafka & Reliability
* **Phase 8** — Security & Compliance
* **Phase 9** — Observability & Operations
* **Phase 10** — AI Anomaly Detection
* **Phase 11** — RAG Payment Operations Copilot
* **Phase 12** — Failure Simulation & Payment Replay Lab
* **Phase 13** — Testing, Performance & CI/CD
